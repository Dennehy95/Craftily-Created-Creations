E-Fishcient Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

E-Fishcient (Android package com.efishcient.app) is a to-do and reminder app by Bryan Dennehy (“the developer”). In short: the app keeps everything on your phone, and the developer collects nothing.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-in and no server.
  • No analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.
  • No data is collected, transmitted or shared with anyone.
  • The app does not request internet permission and does not need a network connection.

Data the app stores

The app stores your tasks, notes, groups, schedules, reminder settings, completion and missed history, diary notes and app settings. All of it is saved only on your device, in a local database inside the app’s private storage. The developer cannot see it or access it.

Permissions the app uses

The app’s Android manifest declares only these permissions, and each one is used only for reminders:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) – to show your task reminders. On Android 13 and later the app asks for this permission.
  • Exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) – to fire reminders at the time you chose. If you don’t allow it, reminders still work but may arrive a few minutes late.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) – to set your reminders again after the phone restarts. The app also resets them after a clock or time-zone change or an app update.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files, or the internet.

Backup export and import

In Settings you can export a backup file and import one later. This is always started by you: the file is saved to, or read from, a location you pick using Android’s file picker. The developer never receives the file. What you do with it (for example, copying it to another device or a cloud drive of your choice) is up to you.

Separately, Android’s own device backup feature (if you have it turned on in your phone settings) may include app data as part of your device backup. That is provided and controlled by Android and your Google account settings, not by this app or the developer.

Deleting your data

You can remove all app data at any time by uninstalling the app or by clearing its storage in Android Settings > Apps > E-Fishcient > Storage. You can also delete tasks inside the app. Because the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing to request from the developer.

Children

E-Fishcient is not directed at children. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page and the “Last updated” date above will change.
